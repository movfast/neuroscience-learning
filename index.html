Neural Signal Transmission

Watch how electrical signals travel through neurons — the fundamental process behind every thought, movement, and sensation.

Action Potential: An electrical impulse (~100 mV) that travels along the axon at speeds up to 120 m/s.
Saltatory Conduction: Signals "jump" between gaps in the myelin sheath (Nodes of Ranvier), dramatically increasing speed.
Synaptic Transmission: At the axon terminal, neurotransmitters are released across the synaptic cleft to the next neuron.
Neurotransmitters: Chemical messengers including dopamine (reward), serotonin (mood), GABA (inhibition), and glutamate (excitation).
